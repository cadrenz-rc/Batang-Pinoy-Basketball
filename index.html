THE PLAYOFF RACE GOES DOWN TO THE WIRE

One undefeated leader. Three teams trying to chase it down. And the battle for the final Division 1 spots is anyone's to fight for.

The clock is ticking on BPBL Season 9. After ten games on October 31, the next game day is November 21, 2026, with the playoffs starting November 28, 2026. Every possession could change the playoff picture. And after this weekend, nobody can pretend the race is settled.

THE KNIGHTS STAND ALONE

Bambhorats Knights are 6–0 after handing Haides X Journey its first loss, 86–70. The Knights are the only unbeaten team remaining, but an unblemished regular season isn't guaranteed yet. Their November 21 matchup is the next major test before the playoffs begin November 28.

THREE CHALLENGERS. ONE CHANCE TO RESPOND.

JMC Alliance dropped a two-point thriller to Slytherin, 67–65. Haides X Journey ran into the unbeaten Knights. Meanwhile, Wild Tribe beat Silent Threat 74–59, with Lordrikk Gutierrez scoring 30 points. All three now sit at 5–1—with seeding positions still at stake.

FIVE TEAMS ON THE BUBBLE

Silent Threat, Pipers, and Epic Grill Silogs are each 3–3. Behind them, Slytherin, L2G, Gentlemen's Club, Fitman Hunter, and Dentio Dental are all 2–4. That's five teams battling around the eighth-seed line. Slytherin holds the current eighth position, but the November 21 results could rearrange the chase.

NEXT GAME DAY: TWO SHOWDOWNS TO WATCH

JMC Alliance vs Bambhorats Knights, 6:30 PM at Middle School Gym: The November 21 headline matchup is here. Bambhorats can close out at 7–0; JMC has a chance to hand the league leaders their first defeat and improve its own position among the 5–1 contenders. Any effect on the official top seeds will depend on the league's tiebreakers.

Slytherin vs Fitman Hunter, 1:30 PM at Middle School Gym: Both enter at 2–4. Slytherin currently holds the eighth seed and Fitman is just below the Division 1 cutoff. A head-to-head result could reshape that border, depending on other scores.

Elsewhere, Dentio Dental meets Haides X Journey, Gentlemen's Club faces Pipers, and Quolos Construction takes on L2G. Those outcomes matter to the crowded middle and lower half of the standings. Epic Grill Silogs vs Wild Tribe and Sando Republika vs Silent Threat give both teams on the bubble and teams chasing higher seeds another opportunity to make their cases.

PLAYOFF WATCH:

Seeds 1–8 enter Division 1, seeds 9–12 enter Division 2, and seeds 13–14 are out. Current rankings are not guaranteed final seeds. We have not assumed the league's tiebreaker order, so we are not declaring any specific team mathematically locked or eliminated.
